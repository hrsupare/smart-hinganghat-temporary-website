Smart Hinganghat – Account & Data Deletion

Smart Hinganghat is committed to protecting user privacy and ensuring transparency in how personal data is handled. Users have the right to request deletion of their account and associated data at any time.

Data Eligible for Deletion

Upon successful verification, the following data associated with your account will be permanently deleted:

  • Registered phone number
  • User account profile details
  • Service requests, complaints, and related records
  • Any other data linked to your Smart Hinganghat account

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your account and associated data, please send an email to the official support address mentioned below. Ensure that you include your registered phone number in the request for verification purposes.

📧 Support Email: admin@urbanmaster.in

Processing Time

Once the request is verified, the account and associated data will be permanently deleted from our systems within 30 days, unless retention is required by applicable laws or regulations.
