Account & Data Deletion

Smart Hinganghat respects your privacy and gives you full control over your personal data. Users can request deletion of their account and associated data at any time.

What data will be deleted

  • Registered phone number
  • User account information
  • Service requests and complaint records
  • Any other data associated with your account

How to request account deletion

To request deletion of your account and associated data, please contact us using the email address below. Make sure to send the request from your registered phone number or mention it clearly in the email.

📧 Email: support@smarthinganghat.in

Processing time

Once your request is verified, we will permanently delete your account and associated data from our systems within 30 days, unless data retention is required by law.
